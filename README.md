## rb15-artist-kit
Artist reference kit for painting liveries on the RB15 F1 Car Mod Demo (Cyberpunk 2077, Nexus 31543):
UV wireframe templates (body atlas + badge/decal plate sheet), colour-coded region maps, mirror map,
orientation probe liveries and the green "NCM TEST" livery source, with notes in `rb15-artist-kit/README.md`.
Also packaged as `RB15_artist_reference_kit_2026-09-18_no-stock-textures.zip`.

The mod author's own textures (stock-reference) are intentionally **not** included here; they stay local.
